questions

when will i get my order?

the pre-order is open till 5 september, then the run goes into production. it takes up to four weeks to make. i will write to you as soon as the pieces are with me, and we will agree how to get yours to you.

how much is shipping?

shipping is not included in the price and is counted separately: it depends on the country and the method. tell me where it should go and i will work it out. in lisbon you can pick it up in person, and then there is no shipping at all.

what if the size doesn’t fit?

the pieces are made to the orders collected, so there are no spare sizes left over. check the measurements in the size chart, and if you are between two sizes, write to me and i will help you choose.

is there another way to pay?

you can pay for the pre-order by card on a secure stripe page, card details never pass through this site, or in cash when we meet in lisbon. if neither works for you, write to me and we’ll find an option that feels right.

what happens after i pay?

i’ll write to you at the contact you left with the order, and we’ll agree on shipping or on meeting up. your order number appears on the page right after payment, no need to keep it, i have it too.

the payment doesn’t go through, what now?

sometimes a bank refuses the payment. nothing is charged when that happens, the order simply isn’t placed. write to me on telegram and i’ll take the order by hand.
