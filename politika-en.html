privacy policy

publication date: 26 august 2026

1. general provisions

this privacy policy governs the processing and protection of personal data of users of the soft power website, softpowerspace.com (the «site»), in accordance with the general data protection regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679.

2. data controller

the controller of personal data is DARIA PROSTOVA (the «controller»).

contact email address for matters related to personal data: softpower.backup@gmail.com.

3. categories of personal data processed

the controller processes the following categories of personal data:

  • name
  • email address
  • phone number
  • telegram username
  • address, if an order has to be shipped

4. methods of data collection

personal data is collected through:

  • forms on the softpowerspace.com site, including the shop order form
  • the stripe payment page the buyer is taken to from the site
  • google forms

5. purposes of processing

users’ personal data is processed for the following purposes:

  • placing an order, handing the piece over and agreeing on delivery
  • delivery of materials related to purchased subscriptions and products
  • organising future events and communicating about them

6. legal grounds for processing

personal data is processed on the basis of:

  • the user’s consent to the processing of their personal data, article 6(1)(a) GDPR
  • the necessity to perform a contract or take steps at the request of the user prior to entering into a contract, article 6(1)(b) GDPR

7. transfer of personal data to third parties

users’ personal data may be transferred to third parties only when necessary to fulfil obligations to the user and solely for the purposes described in this policy.

the stripe payment service receives data for processing payments and providing access to digital products: name, email address, phone number, payment details, address and IP address. stripe processes personal data in accordance with its privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

personal data is transferred only to third parties that ensure an adequate level of data protection in accordance with GDPR requirements.

8. data retention period

personal data is stored only for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes for which it was collected, or until the user withdraws their consent, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable EU law.

9. user rights

in accordance with GDPR, the user has the right to:

  • request access to their personal data
  • request rectification or erasure of personal data
  • restrict or object to the processing of personal data
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

to exercise these rights, the user may contact the controller at softpower.backup@gmail.com.

10. personal data protection measures

the controller implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including protection against unauthorised or unlawful processing and against accidental loss, destruction or damage.

11. changes to the privacy policy

the controller reserves the right to amend this privacy policy at any time. the current version is always available on the site.

12. age restrictions

the site is intended for users who are 18 years of age or older.

if you have questions about your data, write to softpower.backup@gmail.com or on telegram.
